## Questions for management

1. Industrial and Energy account for $3.3m of the $3.4m revenue increase. How much came from volume versus pricing?
2. Did the 1.5-point gross-margin improvement come from lasting efficiencies or temporary input-cost relief?
3. How much of the $18.6m backlog ships next quarter, and what can customers cancel?
